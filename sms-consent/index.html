Everdry Waterproofing of the Fox Cities — SMS Consent Script

Public opt-in evidence for appointment / inspection text messages. No login required. PDF copy: /sms-consent.pdf.

When this is read

Read to every homeowner who schedules a free basement inspection, in person or by phone, before the appointment is finalized:

“We'll confirm your inspection by phone and text message. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out. Is that okay?”

The homeowner’s agreement is recorded on the appointment before submission.

How opt-in works (message flow)

  • In person / door: canvasser or setter reads the script above; homeowner must answer yes before the appointment is submitted.
  • By phone: office or confirmation desk reads the same script before finalizing the free inspection appointment.
  • Website: when a phone number is collected for texts, the form shows equivalent disclosure (message purpose, frequency, rates, HELP/STOP) with an unchecked opt-in next to the phone field.
  • Record: verbal or form yes is kept with the appointment / lead record before outbound appointment texts are sent.

What the texts are for

Appointment and free basement inspection confirmations, reminders, and related customer-care notices from Everdry Waterproofing of the Fox Cities. Message frequency varies (typically a small number per scheduled visit). Message and data rates may apply.

HELP / STOP

  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Opt-out is honored immediately.
  • Reply HELP for help. You may also call the office or visit the site below.
  • After opt-in, a confirmation text may restate brand, frequency, rates, HELP, and STOP.
