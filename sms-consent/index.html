Everdry Waterproofing of the Fox Cities — SMS Consent

How Customers Opt In

Homeowners who schedule a free basement inspection with Everdry Waterproofing of the Fox Cities may choose to receive appointment-related text messages.

Consent is obtained verbally, either in person with an Everdry representative or by phone with our scheduling office, before text messages are sent.

SMS Consent Script

Our representative reads the following disclosure:

“Would you like to receive appointment confirmation, reminder, and scheduling text messages from Everdry Waterproofing of the Fox Cities? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Is that okay?”

The homeowner must affirmatively agree before being enrolled to receive text messages. The homeowner's consent is recorded with the appointment.

Message Information

  • Messages are limited to appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and scheduling updates.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.
  • Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared, sold, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
